# A8 – Gear Box Design

## Objective

The objective of this assignment is to design a gearbox that can be assembled and disassembled. This involves analyzing the input and output shafts to determine whether their diameters provide sufficient strength and designing journal bearings to support the shafts. The assignment also requires designing a gearbox housing with appropriate clearances and fastening methods. Parametric CAD modeling will be used to create the final gearbox design. Throughout the process, calculations, sketches, design decisions, and lessons learned will be documented.

I selected oil-embedded 841 bronze sleeve journal bearings from McMaster-Carr to support the input and output shafts. For the input shaft, I chose bearing 6391K111, which has an inside diameter of 0.126 inches, an outside diameter of 0.252 inches, and a length of 0.250 inches. For the output shaft, I chose bearing 6391K126, which has an inside diameter of 0.2505 inches, an outside diameter of 0.3145 inches, and a length of 0.250 inches. Both bearings are made of oil-embedded bronze, which provides lubrication and helps reduce friction and wear as the shafts rotate. I chose nuts and bolts to affix the different parts of the gearbox together.

I designed the gearbox housing with an overall width of 3.77 inches, a height of 1.89 inches, and an interior width of 3.13 inches to provide enough space for the gears, shafts, and journal bearings. I selected a housing depth of 0.40 inches to accommodate the internal components while keeping the design compact. The input and output shaft holes have diameters of 0.126 and 0.251 inches, respectively, to allow the shafts to rotate. I positioned the shaft holes 0.95 inches below the top of the housing and added four 0.12-inch fastening holes, located 0.30 inches from the top and bottom edges, to secure the two housing halves together. The input and output shafts were modeled with the specified diameters of 0.125 and 0.250 inches and lengths of 1 inch. I copied this design for the back of the gear box housing and just removed the holes for the shafts.
