#About Me

My name is Dina Chouffani, and I am a student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte majoring in Mechanical Engineering with a concentration in Biomedical Engineering. I am most interested in biomedical engineering because I want to create and improve solutions that can positively impact the health and lives of others. I enjoy solving problems, improving existing designs, understanding how physical systems work, and why they work. My strongest skills currently include mathematics, laboratory work, engineering calculations, and CAD design. I especially enjoy hands on work because I find it rewarding to take an idea through the design process and see it become a physical product.

Though mathematics and science have always been my strongest subjects, I genuinely enjoy learning and applying them to solve problems. This led me to mechanical engineering because it allows me to combine mathematics and science with design, problem solving, and hands on work. Mechanical engineering allows me to approach problems systematically, understand the principles behind physical systems, and use that understanding to develop practical solutions.

Research has helped shape the engineer I am becoming. It constantly requires me to think critically, solve unexpected problems, reconsider ideas that do not work, and develop new approaches. These experiences have taught me that engineering is not only about finding a solution, but about continuously evaluating and improving that solution. I hope to build a career in the biomedical side of engineering, where I can improve existing technologies and develop new solutions that benefit human health. I am becoming a detail oriented engineer who values meaningful work and wants to use engineering to make a positive difference in people's lives.
